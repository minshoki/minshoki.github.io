Sky data for Today’s Camp

Download the adapted star catalog and constellation figures under CC BY-SA 4.0. The archive contains the complete catalog, attribution/change notice, and license. No account is required.

Star positions derive from HYG v4.1 by Astronomy Nexus / astronexus. Constellation figures derive from Stellarium’s team’s modern sky culture. Adaptations include brightness filtering, retaining faint endpoints, converting right ascension to degrees, selecting 46 constellation figures, deduplicating segments, adding Korean names and label centroids, and formatting as Unity-compatible JSON. These sources do not endorse the game.
